# Flashcard Review System - How It Works

## 🎯 The Goal
Help you remember everything by showing cards at the perfect time - not too soon (wasting time) and not too late (forgetting).

---

## 📱 How to Review

1. **See the Front** of the card (e.g., "Hello" or "run quickly")
2. **Try to Remember** what's on the back
3. **Tap to Flip** and see the answer
4. **Rate Yourself** with one of 4 buttons

---

## 🔴🟠🟢🔵 The 4 Buttons

### **Again** 🔴
- "I forgot completely" or "I got it wrong"
- **Shows:** Same day (relearn it)

### **Hard** 🟠  
- "I remembered, but it was really difficult"
- **Shows:** Same day at first, then short intervals

### **Good** 🟢
- "I remembered it normally" (most common choice)
- **Shows:** Increases gradually over time

### **Easy** 🔵
- "I knew it instantly, too easy!"
- **Shows:** Much longer intervals

---

## 📅 How the Intervals Work

### **Again** (Red Button)
- Same day → You'll see it again soon in the same session or later today

### **Hard** (Orange Button)
- 1st time: Same day
- 2nd time: 1 day
- After that: Slow growth (×1.2 each time)

### **Good** (Green Button) - **RECOMMENDED**
- 1st time: 1 day
- 2nd time: 3 days
- 3rd time: 7 days
- 4th time: 16 days
- Keeps growing smoothly

### **Easy** (Blue Button)
- 1st time: 2 days
- 2nd time: 5 days  
- 3rd time: 14 days
- Grows faster than "Good"

---

## 🎓 Example: Learning "Hola" = "Hello"

**Day 1** - First time seeing it:
- Flip card, see answer
- Choose: **Good** 🟢
- Next review: **Tomorrow**

**Day 2** - Second time:
- Remember it!
- Choose: **Good** 🟢
- Next review: **3 days**

**Day 5** - Third time:
- Remember it!
- Choose: **Good** 🟢
- Next review: **7 days**

**Day 12** - Fourth time:
- Remember it!
- Choose: **Easy** 🔵 (getting easier!)
- Next review: **20 days**

**Day 32** - Fifth time:
- Can't remember...
- Choose: **Again** 🔴
- Next review: **Same day** (start over)

---

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Be honest** - Don't click "Good" if you struggled!
2. **Use "Again"** freely - It's not a failure, it's learning
3. **"Good" is default** - Use it most of the time
4. **"Easy" is for mastered cards** - Only if instant recall
5. **Review daily** - Even 5 minutes helps!

---

## 🧠 The Science Behind It

This system is based on **proven flashcard research** (Anki, SuperMemo):

- **Spaced Repetition**: Review right before you'd forget
- **Active Recall**: Forces your brain to retrieve information
- **Increasing Intervals**: Strengthens long-term memory
- **Forgetting is Learning**: Struggling helps you remember better

The more times you successfully recall a card, the longer the interval becomes. Cards you struggle with appear more frequently until you learn them.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: What if I keep forgetting a card?**  
A: That's normal! Keep hitting "Again" and it'll show up frequently until you learn it.

**Q: Should I always click "Good"?**  
A: No, be honest! If it was hard, click "Hard". If you forgot, click "Again".

**Q: Can I review the same card multiple times per day?**  
A: Yes! If you click "Again", it'll show up again in the same session.

**Q: What if I accidentally click the wrong button?**  
A: The card will adjust over time. One wrong rating won't hurt.

**Q: How long until I master a card?**  
A: Depends on the card! Easy ones might be mastered in weeks, difficult ones in months.

## 🎯 TL;DR (Too Long; Didn't Read)

1. **Review cards daily**
2. **Flip → Rate honestly**
3. **Use Good (🟢) most of the time**
4. **Don't be afraid of Again (🔴)**
5. **Trust the system!**

The app will take care of scheduling. Just be honest when rating and review regularly!
